# Game Design Notes

## Tone

Comedy-horror, played mostly straight.

The world should look committed to the joke rather than constantly telling the
player that it is a joke. The funniest moments should come from serious FPS
presentation applied to ridiculous retail problems.

## Core fantasy

You are the only employee left on the night shift when the store becomes
hostile.

### Signature ideas

1. **The store fights back**
   - self-checkout machines become enemies,
   - carts charge like animals,
   - pallet jacks patrol warehouse aisles,
   - price scanners act like laser turrets.

2. **Workplace weapons**
   - Emergency Mop,
   - Turbo Can Launcher,
   - Receipt Ripper,
   - Price-Gun SMG,
   - Fire-Exit Extinguisher,
   - Industrial Stapler,
   - Pallet-Jack Ram.

3. **Ridiculous bosses played seriously**
   - Night Manager,
   - Frozen Foods Beast,
   - The Regional Manager,
   - Inventory Auditor,
   - Customer Service Hydra.

## Prototype loop

Enter arena -> survive the shift -> test weapons -> kill the manager.

## Visual direction

Not cute-cartoon. Instead:
- chunky retro-FPS shapes,
- exaggerated retail signage,
- fluorescent lighting,
- unsettling empty-store atmosphere,
- over-the-top hit reactions,
- absurd objects treated as lethal military hardware.

## Originality guardrail

The game may inherit the fast pace of classic FPS design, but should avoid
copying protected characters, logos, maps, textures, audio, story, weapon art,
or enemy designs from Doom, Star Wars, or other commercial IP.
