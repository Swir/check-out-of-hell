# MAP06 — Management Floor

`MAP06 — Management Floor` is playable development content for CHECKOUT OF HELL. It is not a polished final campaign level and does not change the current public-demo gate or weighted project percentage.

## Purpose

Management Floor turns the store's corporate hierarchy into a readable workplace objective sequence instead of a pure arena. The current pass uses paired executive-office wings around a permanently open center corridor, a rear boardroom gate, a fixed Corporate review hold, department-specific deep-Overtime pressure and a boss-scale executive response.

## Current objective route

1. Enter through the front-center staff route with the normal shift kit.
2. Restore the two opposite-side **Executive Access circuits**.
3. At `2/3` power, the two project-owned boardroom shutters open the narrow rear-center doorway.
4. Collect the physical **Boardroom Breaker Authorization**. It does not grant full power directly; it starts a save-safe **10-second Boardroom Review**.
5. Survive the fixed review beat: a Security Price Scanner enters at 4 seconds, a second warning lands at 7 seconds, an Angry Self-Checkout enters at 9 seconds, and only the 10-second completion grants the authoritative final `CheckoutFuse` token.
6. Full authorization arms a separate four-second visual/audio arrival warning before **The District Director** enters the boardroom.
7. Defeating the District Director grants the existing shared supervisor-clearance state. The player then returns through the still-open central route to the existing front clock-out trigger.

The Boardroom Review, District Director arrival and Executive Audit all use serialized actor/inventory state so ordinary save/restore resumes the active department beat instead of inventing a parallel progression ledger.

## Executive Audit Overtime

Management Floor's distinctive Overtime mechanic is the **Executive Audit**. It is intentionally separate from the fixed Boardroom Review.

- It cannot begin until both Executive Access circuits are live and Overtime reaches stage 2.
- The first eligible audit waits 16 seconds.
- Every audit gives a full two-second warning on an authored outer-right office lane.
- Stage 2 answers with a Security Price Scanner; Hell Rush answers with a Cart of Doom.
- Repeat spacing is deterministic at `32 / 22` seconds for Overtime / Hell Rush.
- The ten-second Boardroom Review pauses recurring audits and leaves an eight-second recovery window afterward so authored pressure systems do not stack unfairly.
- The permanent center corridor is never closed by this mechanic.
- Supervisor clearance retires future Executive Audit pressure before the final return-to-clock-out leg.

This keeps the Overtime escalation learnable and readable: the player can anticipate where Corporate pressure arrives without removing the wider hostile store systems.

## District Director

The District Director is now a distinct project-owned corporate boss presentation rather than a Regional Manager reskin. `tools/generate_district_director_assets.py` deterministically generates the full `DDIR A–O` sprite family: a tall executive silhouette with a cyan KPI visor, split cyan/magenta lapels, performance-review clipboard tell and oversized KPI-stamp rage tell. It also generates deterministic project-owned cue families for idle, attack, phase, stamp, pain and death sounds. The four-second boardroom arrival warning uses the same Director-specific phase cue, so the handoff and the fight share one readable audio language.

The actor keeps `RegionalManager` inheritance only for already-tested collision and projectile defaults; every visible actor state uses `DDIR` frames and every boss-owned combat cue uses the `coh/director*` family. The first attack rhythm combines an executive stamp with a three-way Corporate Red Tape volley. Below half health, the Director changes to a five-way paperwork fan plus a paired executive-stamp follow-up. The gameplay remains deterministic and keeps the same 1400-health durability so this presentation pass does not quietly invalidate existing balance assumptions.

The boss actor deliberately does not own fuse or supervisor progression state. A separate Management Floor death handler grants the shared `SupervisorClearanceToken`, keeping post-boss Overtime retirement and clock-out logic authoritative in one existing system.

All boss art and sound are generated inside this repository. No proprietary Doom, Star Wars or other ripped commercial asset is permitted or required.

## Soundtrack

MAP06 uses the sixth deterministic project-owned department theme:

- `D_COH06.mid` — **Management Floor — Synergy Funeral**

The sparse 50 BPM arrangement leaves room for Boardroom Review warnings, Executive Audit telegraphs and boss combat cues. It is generated by `tools/generate_music_assets.py` using only Python's standard library and is packaged under the normal PK3 `music/` namespace.

## Validation

The normal build runs the dedicated Management Floor contract after packaging the PK3:

```text
python tools/build.py
python tools/test_management_floor_contract.py
python tools/test_music_contract.py
```

The contract protects the two-circuit objective, powered shutters, physical authorization, exact `4 / 7 / 9 / 10` Boardroom Review timing, authoritative final-power hand-off, warned District Director arrival, Executive Audit pause/recovery and `32 / 22` cadence, the full generated `DDIR` sprite/audio family, no Regional Manager sprite-frame fallback inside the boss actor, MAP06/MAPINFO/package parity and this implementation note.
